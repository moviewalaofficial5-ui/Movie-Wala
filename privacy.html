Privacy Policy

This page explains, in simple terms, how Movie Wala handles information while you use the website.

Information stored in your browser

Movie Wala may store your TMDB API key and My List data in your browser's local storage. This information is used to make the website's features work on your device.

Search and TMDB requests

When you search or browse titles, the website sends requests to TMDB to retrieve movie and series information. TMDB's own policies and privacy practices apply to information handled by TMDB.

Cookies

The Movie Wala interface does not require cookies for its core movie-list functionality. Your browser may still handle cookies or storage used by third-party services you interact with.

Third-party services

Movie Wala uses TMDB and may display YouTube trailers. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at moviewalaofficial5@gmail.com.
