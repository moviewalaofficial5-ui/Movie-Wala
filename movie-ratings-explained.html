Movie Ratings Explained: What Do They Really Tell You?

Understand how movie ratings can help with discovery without treating a score as an official verdict.

Movie ratings are useful because they provide a quick way to compare titles, especially when you have many options. A high score can indicate that a large group of viewers responded positively, while a lower score may suggest mixed reactions.

But a rating is not the same as a professional review or certification. Different audiences value different things. A viewer who loves action may rate a fast-paced film highly, while another viewer may prefer a slower character study.

Ratings can also change over time as more votes are added. That means the number you see today may not be the same later. It is better to combine the score with genre, premise, cast, release year and your own preferences.
